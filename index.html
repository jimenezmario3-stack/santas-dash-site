Santa's Dash

A one-tap Christmas sleigh arcade game for iPhone and iPad

SupportPrivacy policy

Need help?

Found a bug, have an idea, or need help with the game? Open a support request and we'll get back to you.

Contact support

Support requests are handled on GitHub. You'll need a free GitHub account to post one. Please include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

How to play

  • Tap the screen (or click / press Space on Mac) to make the sleigh fly up.
  • Fly through the gaps between chimneys and icicles. Each gap is a point.
  • Touching a chimney, an icicle or the ground ends the run.

Difficulty levels

Choose a level on the menu with the arrows: Snowflake (extremely easy), Snowman (easy), Sleigh Ride (normal), Blizzard (hard) and Krampus Night (insane). Each level keeps its own best score.

Medals and sleighs

Score 10, 20, 35 or 50 in one run to earn the Candy Cane, Silver Bell, Star and Golden Star medals. Medals unlock new sleighs. Open Sleighs on the menu to see what each one needs. The harder the level, the lower the score you need.

Common questions

Is there no sound? Check the speaker button on the menu and your device's silent switch. On iPhone, the game follows the silent switch.

Did I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app also deletes your best scores and unlocked sleighs.

Does it cost anything? No. Santa's Dash is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
