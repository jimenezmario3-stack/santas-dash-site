Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version

Santa's Dash does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves your best score for each difficulty level, the number of games played, your chosen sleigh, your chosen difficulty, and whether sound is on. This information is stored only on your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is deleted if you delete the app.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, usage data or any other information. The game works fully offline and makes no network connections.

Third parties

Santa's Dash contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software.

If you choose to share crash reports or app usage with developers in your device settings, Apple may provide anonymous diagnostics to us under Apple's privacy policy. This is controlled by you in your device settings.

Children

Santa's Dash is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
